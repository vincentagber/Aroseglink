Canada Study Visa & Work Permit

Canada Study Visa & DLI University Placements

Canada remains one of the world's most attractive destinations for Nigerian students, offering world-class academic institutions, a multicultural society, and clear pathways to permanent residency. Our specialized Canada Admissions desk secures your acceptance at verified Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) and prepares a compliant, compelling study permit application designed for maximum visa approval.

  • Verified Local Agent for Humber College: Officially listed on Humber College's international agent network in Toronto, Ontario.
  • Registered Canadian Office: Operating as Aroseglinks Services Ltd. (CBCA Corporation No: 1425324-8) at 907 Grenoble Blvd, Pickering, ON, ensuring on-ground Canadian support and post-arrival settlement.
  • Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs): Placement across accredited Canadian universities and colleges in Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, and Manitoba.
  • Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP): Earn up to 3 years of Canadian work experience, qualifying you for permanent residency.
  • Airtight Visa Documentation: Rigorous review of Proof of Funds (POF), bank verification, and high-impact Statements of Purpose (SOP).
