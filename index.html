Aroseglinks Zillionaire Group Heirs Holdings

Aroseglinks Zillionaire Group Heirs Holdings, Enterprise Number 2025/071786/07, is a diversified investment and holding company that acquires, builds, and scales durable businesses across Africa, the United States, the UAE, Qatar, and the United Kingdom, with a focus on private equity, venture capital, enterprise IT, AI and innovation, healthcare and biotechnology, agriculture, logistics, electric vehicles, real estate, construction, and financial technology.

The Group combines long-term investment capital with operating insight to identify high-potential businesses, strengthen their capabilities, and accelerate sustainable growth through AI and innovation labs, funding accelerators, R&D hubs, and technology platforms that transform applied innovation into measurable operating advantage.

Its portfolio also extends into theme parks and resorts, developing eco-friendly leisure destinations that integrate entertainment, tourism, and long-term real-asset value; media and film, financing content and entertainment.
